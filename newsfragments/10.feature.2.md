Nodes on [a, b] are one ties-to-even binary64 rounding of (b-a)/2 * x + (a+b)/2. Weights are one ties-to-even binary64 rounding of w * H**(alpha+beta+1), where H is the binary64 half-width fl((b-a)/2) and a flushed H is 2**-1075. A non-finite scaled node or weight is GJP_ERR_BOUNDS (status 7) and leaves both buffers unchanged.
